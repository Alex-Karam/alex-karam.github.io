My background is in theoretical physics, with experience in mathematical modelling, numerical analysis and communicating complex results. I'm now developing practical business and financial analytics skills through portfolio projects.

I focus on the steps that make an analysis understandable and reviewable:

  1. 01Define the question. State what each metric measures and which observations it includes.
  2. 02Check the numbers. Trace reporting results back to their inputs and validation controls.
  3. 03Explain the limits. Separate observed patterns, assumptions and questions that need more evidence.
